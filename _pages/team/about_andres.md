 Andrés Alberto Ramírez-Duque

Andrés Ramírez is a Research Fellow at the University of Glasgow, developing the next generation of electrotactile haptics and other emerging interactive technologies, with a strong focus on hardware design, prototyping, and manufacturing. Andres work at the intersection of hardware engineering, digital fabrication, HCI and responsible technology for children. His research brings together HCI and responsible technology to create tangible experiences that give children and vulnerable populations greater agency in how technologies are designed and used. His work spans electrotactile interfaces, shape-changing tangibles, social robotics and physical data representations, combining hands-on technical development with participatory and child-led design. And when he is not in the lab, he is dad to two children who have a habit of becoming participants, co-designers and research assistants in just about everything he builds.

Nikolaj Gadegaard

I am Professor of Biomedical Engineering in the James Watt School of Engineering at the University of Glasgow. I am also the Dean of post graduate studies in the College of Science and Engineering which has more than 1000 active PhD students. My research background is in micro- and nanoscale engineering with a focus on novel biomaterials. Relevant to BodyElectric, my lab also incorporates biosensing technologies in our work. Here we will specifically focus on bioimpedance analysis and electronic integration of sensing and stimulation.
I am a Fellow of the Royal Society of Edinburgh and previously held an ERC Consolidator Grant (FAKIR).

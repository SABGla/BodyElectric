Patrizia Di Campli San Vito

Dr. Patrizia Di Campli San Vito is a postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Glasgow. Her background is in Human Computer Interaction (HCI), where she has investigated the use of haptic modalities, such as thermal and electrotactile feedback, to provide information to users, and has worked on projects exploring the use of technology to support vulnerable population, such as people with dementia, and participatory auditing of AI applications. She has finished her PhD in Computing Science at the University of Glasgow in 2021, after obtaining both her BSc and MSc in Media Informatics at the University of Ulm, Germany. She was promoted to Research Fellow in 2025.

Frank Pollick

I am a Professor in the School of Psychology and Neuroscience at the University of Glasgow, where I serve as Director of Innovation for the School. My research spans perception, cognitive neuroscience and human-computer interaction. Current research includes multisensory perception, human interaction with technology, trust in human-AI teams, human-robot interaction, and the prediction and mitigation of cybersickness in virtual and extended reality. I use methods ranging from behavioural experiments and motion capture to brain imaging, EEG and real-time fMRI neurofeedback. Within BodyElectric, I contribute expertise in perception and experimental psychology to investigate how electrotactile stimulation is experienced and how it can be used to create comfortable, meaningful and effective tactile sensations. I am also a co-author of the textbook Cognitive Psychology, now in its third edition, an Associate Editor of the International Journal of Humanoid Robotics, and a Consulting Editor for Technology, Mind, and Behavior.

Euan Freeman

I am a lecturer in the School of Computing Science at the University of Glasgow. My research focuses on haptic and immersive interaction techniques, with a particular interest in understanding how we can design quality haptic interfaces that enable users to skillfully express their interaction intentions. I lead a team of four PhD students who work on novel interactions for mixed reality environments. Throughout my research career I have worked extensively with vibrotactile and ultrasound mid-air haptics, and will draw on this background in this project to investigate the design and perception of novel electrotactile feedback.
